# CP10.5 — human QA, one short round (Windows / Dusklight 2.0.3 / DX12)
1. Close the game. Back up `BokoblinExpansion_0.6.0_TEST.dusk` and `encounters.json` (Black = 440). Put ONLY `BokoblinExpansion_0.7.0_TEST.dusk` in its place; keep provider 0.10.0, the editor and your JSON untouched.
2. Start and play to the test room. In the log expect, per Bokoblin, `DEVF_CP10_4_VARIANT ... VARIANT_ADMITTED` (as 0.6.0) **and** one `DEVF_CP10_5_DAMAGE event=DAMAGE_PROFILE_APPLIED`:
   - native `E_OC` (if you meet one): `source=NATIVE_E_OC classified_variant=BLUE collider0_before=1 collider0_after=1 collider1_before=1 collider1_after=1 target_base=1 written=NO`
   - native `E_OC2`: `source=NATIVE_E_OC2 classified_variant=RED ... before=2 after=2 target_base=2 written=NO`
   - DEVF Normal (`bokoblin-001/002/003`): `source=DEVF_NORMAL classified_variant=BLUE ... before=2 after=1 target_base=1 written=YES`
   - Black `bokoblin-004`: `source=DEVF_BLACK classified_variant=BLACK ... before=2 after=3 target_base=3 written=YES`, 440 HP and the dark texture as before. Never `after=4` or `after=6`.
3. **Damage comparison, same conditions every time:** no shield raised, same tunic/armor, enough hearts, and no red-flash invincibility from an earlier hit (wait it out). Take the first hit from one Normal (Blue) and note the hearts/quarters lost; do the same with a Black (and, if you meet one, a native Red). Repeat 3-4 times each; compare hit against hit. Expected from the source: Black loses 3x what Normal loses, Red 2x. If the UI cannot show quarters clearly, record a short video of the hearts (do not estimate): a figure that cannot be read is reported as **not validated**. Anything that changes Link's own damage (Wolf form, armor, a difficulty/damage setting) must be the same in every hit.
4. Shield: with the shield raised and facing it, block a Normal and a Black attack. Expected: the block works as before, no heart is lost and nothing changes in the reaction. The Bokoblin staring at you afterwards is the known native behaviour and is out of scope.
5. Also check: a Bokoblin dies normally, leave/re-enter the room (new `DAMAGE_PROFILE_APPLIED` for the new actors, none repeated for the same one), HP and regeneration as in 0.6.0 (Blue 220/2, Red 330/3, Black 440/4 per second).
6. Send only the `DEVF_CP10_5_DAMAGE`, `DEVF_CP10_4_VARIANT` and `DEVF_CP10_3_REGEN` lines plus your hearts lost per hit (or the video). Keep the full log in case we need it.
7. Rollback at any wrong damage: close the game, restore `BokoblinExpansion_0.6.0_TEST.dusk` and your backed-up JSON; never keep both Expansion files.
