## 2. Mechanism
`strokes_policy.hpp` (pure, templated over a small engine adapter, so the mod and the lab run the same code): `resource_for(variant)` -> `0x0A58` Blue, `0x0B9D` Black, nothing else (Red/unclassified/forbidden ids are refused before any engine call; `never_used` = SWL_B `0x0A59`, WindLine `0x0046`, `0x8046`, glow `0x072F`). `AuraPart::aura`: `REQUESTED` (once per acquisition episode) -> `probe(id)` -> `ready()` (tevStr) -> `set(id, key, x, y, z)` -> `configure(...)` (per-emitter colour/alpha/scale with read-back) -> `EMITTER_ACQUIRED` + `COLOR_APPLIED|UNAVAILABLE`. Any refusal is "pending": the logical state stays on, retried every tick for 60 ticks, then rejected once for that generation. The fade/ownership controller is the unchanged CP10.6A `devf_vis::step`; aura and eye faults are independent; at most 8 emitters, one per actor.
- **Position:** the middle of the body (`current.pos` to `eyePos`), refreshed every tick through the keyed `set()`. **Rotation:** none passed, so identity (documented above).
- **Scale (first-round values, one table):** emission volume and velocities x3.0 (`setGlobalDynamicsScale`, ring radius 24 x 3 = 72, about a Bokoblin's width); particle size x0.45 (`setGlobalParticleScale`: about 9 x 126 units). Colour per emitter: Blue (70,170,255), Black (185,90,255), multiplied with the resource's own colour (logged: `prm_rgba` about (255,255,195,120/150)); alpha: CP10.6A caps 110/130 boosted x1.8. No shared JPAResource is modified; whether the multiplication shows as cyan/violet on screen is not verified.
- **Cleanup:** an emitter nobody refreshes is removed by the engine (`level_c::execute`); we also `stopDrawParticle()` at fade completion. Death, replacement, room change, deactivation and unload therefore leave nothing; the eyes keep their CP10.6A ownership-checked restore.

## 3. Logs (`DEVF_CP10_6C_STROKES`, bounded: 24 lines per actor shared with the eye lines, 400 per session, transitions only)
`RESOURCE_PROBE` (per id: found/missing reason, banks, user work, flags, volume/shape/size, max frame, life, rate, velocity, emitter direction, colours, **key ids/loop/count, extra-shape scale animation**), `RESOURCE_FOUND` / `RESOURCE_MISSING`, `ACTOR_STATE_ON` / `ACTOR_STATE_OFF` (logic), `REQUESTED`, `EMITTER_ACQUIRED`, `EMITTER_REJECTED` (transient with `retries=1`, or permanent), `COLOR_APPLIED` / `COLOR_UNAVAILABLE` (read-back), `EMITTER_RELEASED`. Every line carries variant, source, encounter id, actor id/generation, state, level, alpha, the exact resource, emitter key, emitters used/max, target and read-back colour, scales and `pixels=NOT_VERIFIED`. The eye lines keep `DEVF_CP10_6A_VISUAL`. `PARTICLES_ACTUALLY_VISIBLE` and `HUMAN_VISUAL_ACCEPTED` are never emitted.

## 5. Not verified
The look (vertical strokes vs sword streaks, size, brightness, tint); the effect of the 2 key curves over time; the loss of the A+B+C composition; behaviour in cutscenes; the shared pool under load; other mods hooking `daE_OC_c::execute` or particles.
