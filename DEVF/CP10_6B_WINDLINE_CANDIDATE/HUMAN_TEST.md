# CP10.6B — human QA, one short round (Windows / Dusklight 2.0.3 / DX12)
Scene where the Expansion Bokoblins already live (verified in earlier rounds): stage `F_SP121`, room 3, layer 6 (`bokoblin-001/002/003` Normal = Blue, `bokoblin-004` Black, 440 HP). No setup is needed beyond what you already have; a spawn registered in the log (`REQUEST_ACCEPTED`) is not a spawn observed: look at the Bokoblins themselves.
1. Close the game. Keep a copy of `BokoblinExpansion_0.8.1_TEST.dusk` and `encounters.json`. Remove ONLY `BokoblinExpansion_0.8.1_TEST.dusk` and put `BokoblinExpansion_0.8.2_TEST.dusk` in its place. Keep provider 0.10.0 and the editor 0.10.2; do not duplicate `dev.bokoblin.expansion`; do not touch saves or the JSON.
2. Start; the log must show D3D12 and `bokoblin_expansion` 0.8.2 loaded. Reach the Bokoblins. The first time a Blue or Black ability needs the aura you will see `DEVF_CP10_6B_WINDLINE event=RESOURCE_PROBE ... ok=1 ...` (or `ok=0 reason=...`): keep that line, it describes the real WindLine resource.
3. **Blue alone** (no other Bokoblin within ~650 horizontal / 250 vertical): no aura lines, no `BLUE_ON`.
4. **Blue with an ally nearby, same room** (the logic line `BLUE_GROUP_ON` fires): `BLUE_ON` and `COLOR_APPLIED` lines, and the WindLine aura fades in around the Blue. Walk the ally away or defeat it (`BLUE_GROUP_OFF`): the lines fade out and `BLUE_OFF` appears. If a line says `EMITTER_REJECTED ... retries=1` followed by `EMITTER_ACQUIRED`, the engine refused once at spawn and the retry worked (expected for a Blue that spawns already grouped).
5. **Black outside fury**: no aura, native (non-purple) eyes. **Hit it without killing it** (`BLACK_FURY_START`): `BLACK_ON`, violet aura lines and purple eyes (`BLACK_EYES_ON`). **Hit it again during the fury** (`BLACK_FURY_REFRESH`): nothing should flicker or restart. Wait ~10 s of game time after the last hit (`BLACK_FURY_END`): aura off (`BLACK_OFF`) and native eyes back (`BLACK_EYES_OFF`).
6. **Cleanup:** defeat a Black or a grouped Blue while the effect is on, change room, leave and re-enter: no lines may stay behind, other Bokoblins stay native, a new Bokoblin must not start with an aura (`EMITTER_RELEASED`, `VISUAL_RELEASED` lines are expected).
7. **Red** (native `E_OC2`, if you meet one) gets no aura. HP, regeneration and damage behave as in 0.8.1; the shield and the known "shield stall" are unchanged.
8. Look at the effect with the camera still and while moving it, at normal distance and close. Record a short video or screenshots of: Blue alone vs grouped, Black before / during / after the fury, and the same moments with 0.8.1 for comparison. What I need to know: do the strokes look like thin lines in the air, what direction do they move, how big/bright are they, is the tint visible (cyan / violet) or is the native colour shown, is there any pop, flicker or stray line?
9. Send the full log (at least every `DEVF_CP10_6B_WINDLINE` and `DEVF_CP10_6A_VISUAL` line) plus the capture. Only you can say whether it looks acceptable: this build is a candidate, not an approved look.
10. **Rollback** on a crash, a regression or an unsatisfying look: close the game, remove `BokoblinExpansion_0.8.2_TEST.dusk` and restore exactly the 0.8.1 `.dusk` (and your original `encounters.json` if you changed it). Never keep two Expansion files. Nothing depends on editing saves.
