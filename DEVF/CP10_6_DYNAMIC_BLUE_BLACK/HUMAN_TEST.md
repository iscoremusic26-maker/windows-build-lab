2. **Blue group bonus.** Stand where a Normal Blue (`bokoblin-001/002/003`) has no other Bokoblin within 650 horizontal / 250 vertical units: its `DEVF_CP10_5_DAMAGE ... APPLIED` shows `target_base=1` and no `BLUE_GROUP_*` line. Bring another living Bokoblin into range (any: Blue, Red or the Black): expect `DEVF_CP10_6_DYNAMIC event=BLUE_GROUP_ON ... atp_from=1 atp_to=2 collider0_now=2 collider1_now=2 companion_id=...`. The placed Bokoblins of room 3 may simply be farther apart than 650; "no `BLUE_GROUP_ON` in that scene" is then the correct result, not a failure. Do not edit `encounters.json` to test this. If you need two Blues close together, use a **copy** of the JSON (or the editor on a copy), keep the original backed up, and restore it afterwards.

Regeneration alone must never produce a START. The log records "HP dropped in the native execute", not who hit it. Do not die or restart dozens of times to test this; a few exchanges are enough.
